Run the end-of-day rollup as Chief of Staff. Keep it short.

1. **Moved today** — completed, per business. Name the deliverable, not the
   effort.
2. **Stalled** — what didn't move and the actual reason. If it's waiting on
   her, say so directly.
3. **Waiting on someone else** — who, what, and how long it's been.
4. **Drafts awaiting her send** — list them so nothing sits unnoticed.
5. **Tomorrow's three** — the three things that matter most across all three
   businesses, in priority order per `docs/CHIEF_OF_STAFF.md`.

Do not restate the morning brief. Do not report a task complete when a
specialist returned partial work — say what landed and what didn't.
